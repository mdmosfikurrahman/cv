\section{Professional Experience}

\begin{datedlist}
\item[2024 -- Present] \textbf{Engineering Team Lead} (Software Engineer L-II),
  Akij iBOS Ltd., Dhaka\\
  Architected a multi-tenant travel platform of eight .NET~9 microservices, from an empty
  repository to three live deployments serving 742 B2B agency accounts. Designed the
  flight-search aggregation layer, fanning out over gRPC to eleven supplier and GDS
  connectors (Sabre, Amadeus, Travelport) over Server-Sent Events: 1,000+ fares in under ten
  seconds, the first in approximately four. Modelled pricing and discount logic as tenant
  configuration, moving 96 live rules out of the deploy path. Built the observability layer
  capturing every inter-service call, now the primary production diagnosis tool. Led a team
  of ten.

  Delivered the Legal Case and Utilization Declaration modules of the National Board of
  Revenue's Customs Bond Management System in Java, Spring Boot and Oracle, replacing a
  manual compliance process for garment exporters. Improved system throughput by
  approximately 25\% through query tuning and data-access optimisation.

\item[2022 -- 2023] \textbf{Software Engineer} (Backend), BJIT Group, Dhaka\\
  Built a GraphQL Backend-for-Frontend for Rakuten e-commerce modules, cutting nested-query
  latency through resolver batching and schema stitching.
\end{datedlist}
